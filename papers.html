Papers

Documentation requirements and optional publication opportunities for AMPLIFAI participating teams.

Important distinction: The Public Method Overview is mandatory for every participating team and is required for AMPLIFAI challenge-award eligibility. Submission of a Springer LNCS proceedings paper is optional and provides a separate opportunity for peer-reviewed publication, presentation at the AMPLIFAI Satellite Event, and consideration for the Best Proceedings Paper Award.

Public Method Overview

Required

MICCAI requires challenge organizers to ensure that a description of each participant’s submission is publicly available.

To satisfy this requirement, every participating AMPLIFAI team must submit a Public Method Overview describing the method associated with its final official challenge submission.

A valid Public Method Overview is required for eligibility for AMPLIFAI challenge awards.

Submission requirements

Each team must provide at least one final Method Overview.

The document should:

  • Follow an extended-abstract style.
  • Be approximately four pages.
  • Be understandable without access to private source code.
  • Describe the method associated with the team’s latest official challenge submission.
  • Include the associated challenge submission ID and submission date.
  • Identify all external datasets, pretrained models, foundation models, pseudo-labels, and other external resources used.
  • Be reviewed and approved by the submitting team for public release.

Teams may submit an initial version and update it later. The final publicly released version must accurately describe the method used for the team’s final official challenge submission.

Recommended structure

  1. Team and Submission Information
    • Team name
    • Team members
    • Affiliations
    • Submission ID
    • Submission date
  2. Introduction
  3. Method
    • Model architecture
    • Data preprocessing
    • Training procedure
    • Inference and postprocessing
    • External data and pretrained resources
  4. Results and Discussion
  5. Limitations
  6. Conclusion
  7. References
  8. Disclosure
    • External-data declaration
    • Pretrained-model declaration
    • Confirmation that the document is approved for public release

Public availability

The Method Overview will be made publicly available through the AMPLIFAI website, OpenReview, or another public challenge repository.

Submission of a Springer proceedings paper does not replace the requirement to provide a final Method Overview unless the organizers explicitly confirm that the proceedings submission contains all required information and accurately describes the team’s final challenge method.

Springer LNCS Proceedings Papers

Optional

AMPLIFAI participants are invited to submit an optional short paper for consideration in the MICCAI 2026 Satellite Event Springer LNCS proceedings.

Submitting a Springer proceedings paper is not required for challenge participation or AMPLIFAI award eligibility. All teams must still submit the mandatory Public Method Overview described above.

Accepted proceedings papers will be published through the MICCAI Satellite Event proceedings process.

Intent to submit

Teams planning to submit a proceedings paper must complete the Intent-to-Submit form by August 10, 2026.

The intent form collects the provisional paper title, author list, contact information, and reviewer conflicts.

Complete the AMPLIFAI Proceedings Intent-to-Submit Form

Submitting the intent form is not the same as submitting the manuscript. Titles and author information may be updated before the initial manuscript deadline.

Paper format

Proceedings submissions must:

  • Use the Springer LNCS template.
  • Be 6–11 pages in total.
  • Include the title, abstract, figures, tables, acknowledgments, disclosures, appendices, and references within the page limit.
  • Contain significant scientific and technical content.

There is no fixed numerical limit on figures or tables. However, all content must fit within the page limit and comply with Springer’s requirements for clarity, legibility, captions, and image quality. Springer requires figure text to remain legible and recommends vector graphics for diagrams where possible.

Expected manuscript content

The paper should describe the team’s latest implemented AMPLIFAI method as of the initial manuscript deadline, including:

  • Overall methodology and model architecture.
  • Data preprocessing.
  • Training strategy and loss functions.
  • Inference and postprocessing.
  • Use of external datasets or pretrained models.
  • Validation experiments and relevant ablation studies.
  • Reproducibility and implementation details.
  • Limitations and known failure modes.

Teams may revise their manuscript in response to reviewer feedback before the camera-ready deadline.

The camera-ready submission must be final. Springer does not permit substantive changes to results, methods, titles, or authorship during the later proofing stage.

Review process

Each submission will be evaluated using:

  • Two reviewers.
  • One meta-reviewer.
  • Final decisions by the AMPLIFAI Proceedings Chairs.

Proceedings acceptance is independent of final challenge leaderboard performance.

Presentation opportunities

Selected accepted papers will be invited for presentation at the AMPLIFAI Satellite Event at MICCAI 2026, alongside the final challenge winners.

The strongest accepted submission will be recognized with an AMPLIFAI Best Proceedings Paper Award.

The following are separate decisions:

  • Proceedings-paper acceptance.
  • Oral or poster presentation selection.
  • Best Proceedings Paper selection.
  • Final challenge ranking.
  • Challenge-award selection.

Camera-ready requirements for accepted papers

Accepted-paper authors must provide:

  • Final manuscript PDF.
  • Complete LaTeX or Microsoft Word source files.
  • Bibliography files.
  • Figures and associated source materials.
  • One supplementary PDF, if applicable.
  • Final title and abstract.
  • Final author list and affiliations.
  • Corresponding-author name and email.
  • ORCIDs, where available.
  • Signed Springer licence-to-publish form.

Springer requires one clearly identified corresponding author. The corresponding author must sign the licence-to-publish form on behalf of all authors. The author list, author order, and corresponding author must be final before delivery to Springer.
